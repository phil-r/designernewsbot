# Designer News

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The photo wall is for people discovering design work on the web. They don't need to follow the Designer News Telegram channel or know which project they're looking for.

## Product Purpose

Help visitors find a Behance project or Dribbble shot worth a closer look, then open the original work. Telegram is another way to follow the feed, not a prerequisite for browsing.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The wall shows only archived posts with saved HTTPS previews, newest first. Each item includes its title, source, and a link to the work.
- The wall is not a complete posting history: a post without a preview can still have a working short link.
- If no archived posts have previews, the wall shows an empty state. It does not fetch work from a live feed.

## Product Principles

- Make discovery useful on the first visit, without Telegram context.
- Keep the source of each work clear and the original project within reach.
